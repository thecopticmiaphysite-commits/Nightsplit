# NIGHTSPLIT creative direction

The original `NIGHTSPLIT-notes.txt` remains the director brief. The later GO MODE
instruction adds a small polished-region-first workflow, unmistakable atmosphere,
story mode and replayable co-op survival in the same original canon, and subtle
evidence planted before later revelations. This is a direction, not a claim that
the current slice implements the finished game.

Relay Nine introduces a listening array landmark, an intake gate, a low service
route, a sealed extension lift and an unreachable industrial skyline. Three
small clue props carry stable RN-01/RN-02/RN-03 identifiers. Future map extensions
should preserve these identifiers and pay off earlier evidence through gameplay.

Maintain the true-history bible and clue ledger privately in `.private/`, excluded
from this public repository. Track chronology, competing interpretations, faction
motives, character secrets, reveal prerequisites, evidence and gameplay consequences.
Never put spoiler truth in replicated modules, attributes, public logs or this doc.
That local private material needs a separate private backup before moving machines.

Use layered truths, delayed reinterpretation and environmental investigation as
storytelling principles. Characters, symbols, locations, weapons and plot events
must remain original. Hints can sit on ordinary traversal routes without announcing
themselves as secrets. Each major reveal needs earlier supporting evidence.

Preserve the director's powerups and paid-area gates requirement: earned run
currency creates a choice between personal strength and opening routes for the
team. Relay Nine tests that tradeoff with Reprieve, Insurance and the intake gate.
Physical stations, Fracture risk/reward, Echo rescue missions, classes and original
signature weapons remain core pillars. No Robux gambling or pay-to-win purchases.

Expand Breaknight through configurable enemy, route, hazard, loot and story events.
The slice currently changes atmosphere, enemy tempo/speed and extraction access;
later regions should prove richer transformations before adding breadth.
